Discovery register only. None of the entries below is approved for bundling yet.

## Acceptance policy

A model may be bundled only when all of these checks pass:

1. The exact asset page offers a downloadable source file.
2. The asset's individual license permits modification and redistribution in a public repository, including commercial use (CC0 or CC BY; CC BY-SA only if we deliberately accept its share-alike obligations).
3. The author, source URL, exact license URL/version and required attribution are recorded.
4. The uploader can reasonably demonstrate that they created the model and had rights to license it. Re-uploads, game rips, extracted configurator assets and assets with unclear provenance are rejected.
5. The vehicle's underlying appearance/design and any visible trademarks/logos are separately reviewed. A Creative Commons license from the 3D uploader does not grant third-party trademark, patent or design rights.
6. The model can be rendered to the C10 gallery standard: front three-quarter view, nose pointing right, full vehicle, transparent background, consistent 1200x675 composition.

## Explicit exclusions

- BYD ETP3, BYD Sealion 7, BYD Atto 2, BYD Dolphin, BYD Seagull, BYD Seal U DM-i and BYD Han DM-p assets by Ddiaz Design found during this search are marked CC BY-NC or CC BY-NC-SA. They are excluded because the non-commercial condition is incompatible with the project's redistribution policy.
- Do not use generic or low-poly cars as substitutes for named catalogue vehicles.
- Do not use CarImage.dev or other sources whose terms prohibit bulk downloading, mirroring or standalone redistribution without a separate agreement.

## Legal and technical notes

- CC BY permits sharing and adaptation, including commercial use, provided attribution and license notices are preserved. It does not itself grant trademark, patent or third-party design rights.
- EU designs can protect the appearance of a product; registered EU designs can last up to 25 years and unregistered EU designs for 3 years. A third-party uploader's CC license is not proof that the uploader owns those rights.
- Sketchfab's terms place responsibility for third-party rights in uploaded models on the uploader. The project must therefore treat the license badge as a necessary but insufficient check.
- For each accepted render, retain a sidecar record with original model URL, creator, license/version, license URL, download date, attribution text, modifications, source-file checksum and rights-review status.
